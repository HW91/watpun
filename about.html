OUR STORY

A Thai temple in the heart of Brevard County

Wat Punyawanaram of Melbourne was incorporated as a nonprofit in 1991. For over three decades it has been a home for the Thai community and a welcoming place for anyone curious about Buddhism.

Our mission is to preserve Thai Buddhist traditions, offer teachings and meditation, and share Thai culture with our neighbors. (Sample text. The temple will provide the final wording.)
