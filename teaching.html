BUDDHIST BASICS

Ideas to start with

The Four Noble Truths

Life includes suffering, suffering has causes, it can end, and there is a path to its end.

The Eightfold Path

Right view, thought, speech, action, livelihood, effort, mindfulness and concentration.

The Five Precepts

Simple promises: do no harm, take only what is given, speak truthfully, act with care and keep a clear mind.
