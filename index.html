EVERY SUNDAY

Sunday Thai Food Market

A warm community gathering with authentic Thai dishes, a Sunday buffet and market goodies from friendly vendors. It is the easiest way to experience Thai culture, and everyone is welcome.

Sundays, 9:00 AM until about noon

  • Come early. The food is popular and sells out quickly.
  • Cash only. Vendors do not accept credit cards.
  • Bring a reusable bag or containers to take food home.

Thai Sart Day

Sunday, September 13, 2026 · Ceremony starts 10:00 AM

Make merit and dedicate the merit to departed relatives and loved ones. 10:00 AM The ceremony chair lights incense and candles. Homage to the Triple Gem and receiving the precepts. 10:30 AM Bang Sukun memorial ceremony. Dedication of merit to those who have passed away. 11:00 AM Alms offering to the monks. Monks chant the auspicious blessing. Lunch offering, merit dedication and blessing. 12:30 PM Dhamma sermon during the Rains Retreat. Evening chanting and meditation. All are warmly invited to join us in this merit-making ceremony.

Thai Mother's Day

Sunday, August 23, 2026 · Ceremony starts 9:00 AM

In Thailand, Mother's Day and the Queen Mother's birthday are celebrated on the same day. Thais use this special day to bestow blessings upon the Queen Mother and to make merit for mothers who love their children. Please join us to celebrate our Thai Mother's Day. 9:00 AM Ceremony begins. 10:00 AM The Royal Merit-Making Ceremony dedicated to Her Majesty Queen Sirikit. 10:30 AM Buddhists recite the Five Precepts and the Monk's Chant. 10:50 AM Collects alms by monks. 11:00 AM Lunch offering to monks. 12:30 PM Sermon by monk, chanting and meditation.
