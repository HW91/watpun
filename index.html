EVERY SUNDAY

Sunday Thai Food Market

A warm community gathering with authentic Thai dishes, a Sunday buffet and market goodies from friendly vendors. It is the easiest way to experience Thai culture, and everyone is welcome.

Sundays, 9:00 AM until about noon

  • Come early. The food is popular and sells out quickly.
  • Cash only. Vendors do not accept credit cards.
  • Bring a reusable bag or containers to take food home.
